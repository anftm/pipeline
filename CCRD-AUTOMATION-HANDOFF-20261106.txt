Why not enable the existing workflow
------------------------------------
.github/workflows/publish-ccrd-index.yml remains disabled. Its legacy writer is
for two sources; update_ccrd_source_txt.py also calls migrate_space(), can move
or delete legacy CW subdirectories and stages all CW. Current four-source guards
are designed to reject legacy publication, not to provide the requested updater.
Do not enable/retry it, do not invoke publish_ccrd_newspapers.py as a substitute,
and do not reactivate old remote OCR.

Work remaining before production activation
--------------------------------------------
1. Implement a real bounded updater/writer based on a fresh single TXT snapshot;
   preserve other source bytes, metadata, paths and source URL provenance.
   Existing local code is a model, not a complete operational implementation.
2. Reconcile the four-source manifest and garbage collection. The current v2
   lifecycle contract requires metadata and 52 newspaper archives under the
   new generation. The one-hop archive reuse shadow is only a synthetic test;
   production GC currently rejects those cross-generation references. Verify
   every preserved reference and design either safe reuse or safe relocation.
3. Prove real-scale CW candidate construction and bounded resource use. The
   in-memory prototype repeatedly reconstructs SQLite and has not been run on
   the real 796 MB database. A checksum of the old database is not a successful
   candidate build or a complete non-target semantic validation.
4. Establish writer isolation, coordinated Space/index activation, durable
   receipts and explicit recovery for timeouts/partial failures. An ETag is not
   a monotonic version or proof against ABA; no VersionId was observed. A
   response timeout does not authorize blind retry. Consumer status/counts alone
   are not an exact loaded-byte receipt. Validate new TXT and four-source search
   against the running consumer after publication.
5. Re-read live main, workflows, run states, HF/Pages commits and other sessions'
   ownership immediately before any push or activation. The parallel PDF route
   migration had required central workflows to remain stopped; coordinate with
   its owner rather than changing that invariant. Enable only a reviewed entry
   after all other protections and permissions are verified.
